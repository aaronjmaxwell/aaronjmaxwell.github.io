Aaron J. Maxwell

I'm an Astrophysicist, Data Scientist, and Story Teller.

I am so glad you are here. Below you can learn a little more about myself.

Education

I wanted to be an astronaut, but astronomy was the next best thing. I received my PhD from McMaster University, where I researched how the dark matter density distribution of galaxies evolves as stars are formed within dwarf galaxies. Throughout my studies I developed an interest in knowledge translation and science policy. I spent a year as vice president of the Graduate Student Association, where I was responsible for health policy.

Work

My experiences during my PhD led me to develop my skills in machine-learning using large datasets. I spent a year working at the Council of Canadian Academies. I used my expertise in coding and data visualization to help empower two Council reports. One of these reports, which I was privileged enough to work on to completion, was on scientific and industrial research in Canada. I spent several years at Paladin AI building an AI system to assist in the training of aircraft pilots. I now work as a Data Analyst at the Natural Sciences and Engineering Research Council of Canada, where I was a Mitacs Canadian Science Policy Fellow.

Science Communication & Outreach

Over the years, I have volunteered for various organizations, including the Canadian Science Policy Conference and Canada Learning Code. During my PhD I volunteered with Let's Talk Science, delivering science outreach programs to historically-excluded groups in remote communities. As a science communicator, I strive to increase representation, access and inclusion. I was a science presenter on the TVOntario show “Blynk & Aazoo” for its two seasons.
